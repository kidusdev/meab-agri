Privacy Policy & Legal Information

Transparency, trust, and accountability in all our business practices

Privacy Policy

We respect client data and ensure that all business and personal information collected is handled with strict confidentiality and used only for agreed purposes.

Information We Collect

MEAB PLC collects information necessary to provide our agricultural services and maintain business relationships:

  • Business Information: Company details, farming operations, and service requirements
  • Contact Information: Names, phone numbers, email addresses, and physical addresses
  • Technical Data: Farming specifications, soil analysis results, and crop performance data
  • Financial Information: Payment details and transaction history for services rendered

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used exclusively for:

  • Providing agricultural products and services
  • Technical support and agronomic consultation
  • Order processing and delivery coordination
  • Quality assurance and product improvement
  • Regulatory compliance and safety standards

Data Protection Commitment

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. All data is encrypted during transmission and stored securely.

Information Sharing

MEAB PLC does not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share information only when:

  • Required by law or regulatory authorities
  • Necessary to provide requested services (with your consent)
  • Required for safety and quality assurance purposes

Terms and Conditions

All transactions, partnerships, and collaborations with MEAB PLC are conducted under clear, fair, and transparent terms, ensuring trust and accountability.

Service Agreement

By engaging with MEAB PLC services, you agree to:

  • Provide accurate and complete information about your farming needs
  • Follow recommended application guidelines for products
  • Maintain proper safety protocols during product use
  • Comply with local agricultural regulations and standards

Product Quality and Warranty

MEAB PLC guarantees:

  • All products meet international quality standards
  • Proper storage and handling conditions maintained
  • Accurate labeling and safety information provided
  • Technical support for optimal product performance

Payment Terms

Payment terms are clearly defined for all transactions:

  • Advance payment required for custom orders
  • Standard credit terms available for established customers
  • All prices include applicable taxes and delivery charges
  • Payment methods: bank transfer, cash, and approved credit terms

Partnership Principles

We believe in building long-term relationships based on mutual trust, transparency, and shared success. All partnerships are governed by clear agreements that protect both parties' interests.
